Owner: marketer · Researched: 2026-10-12 · Status: first pass

**How we checked:** Ratings and in-app purchase (IAP) prices come from the **US App Store** listing as fetched on 2026-10-12, unless a row says otherwise. Google Play ratings and EU/Greek storefront prices were **not checked**. The App Store lists IAP prices without saying which plan each belongs to, so we matched prices to plans by inference. Treat anything marked ⚠ as unverified.

## 2. Per-competitor notes

**Closest direct competitors (shared dog care):**
- **Pawlo.** This is the nearest match to our concept: a live shared checklist that names who did each task and blocks duplicates. It is very new (7 ratings), iOS only, English only, and now free. It has **no claiming or assigning ("I'll take the evening walk")** and no per-person reminders. *Watch closely.*
- **Meal Time For Pets.** It is mature but small (46 ratings), and centred on meals. Family sharing is behind a paywall, with seats capped at 2 or 5. Its prices show what indie developers charge for paid household sharing: **$20–30/yr**.
- **Fed?** Tightly focused on feeding. It uses iCloud, which **excludes Android family members**, a real weakness in mixed-OS households, Greece included. Its Pro tier anchors at **$14.99/yr, or $39.99 lifetime**.
- **Long tail (Kibbl, PawLog, DogLog, PawPact, Peanutty, PetPilot, PiwPaw, PetNote Family).** Many small, recent, mostly iOS-only apps. They show that demand exists but no app has won the category, and none has meaningful review volume. The category idea goes back at least to **DogSync (2015)**.

**Pet health and training (adjacent, single-user):**
- **11pets.** A health-record depth benchmark. It is poorly rated (3.3), and reviews complain about the move to subscription and about paying to add more than one pet.
- **Dogo / Puppr.** These dominate dog-app review volume, but they are training content for one user. Puppr's price (**$99.99/yr**) shows that dog owners will pay for premium content. They do not solve coordination.
- **PetDesk / Pawprint.** Vet-facing records and appointment reminders. They are a possible future partnership or integration, not a competitor for the daily routine.
- **Tractive.** Hardware plus subscription. It owns "where is my dog", not "who walked the dog". It is a possible co-marketing channel in the EU.

**Family chore apps (substitutes):**
- **Cozi / FamilyWall.** Huge install bases. A family can model dog tasks as recurring to-dos, which makes these our main *"good enough"* substitute. They are general-purpose, though, with no dog profile, no meds or vet context, and no claim → done flow per pet task. Cozi drew backlash in 2024 when it limited the free calendar to 30 days ahead, and its free tier shows ads.
- **Tody / Sweepy.** Well loved (4.7–4.8) and cleaning-specific. Both put **household sharing behind the paywall** (Sweepy $19.99/yr; Tody Family $39.99). Users accept paying for sharing in this category. Sweepy has known bugs with recurrence and holiday mode, so recurrence must be rock solid.
- **Homey / S'moresUp / OurHome.** Kid-reward chore apps. Some families put "feed the dog" in them as a kid chore. Their pattern of giving points to kids is worth borrowing later.

## 3. Greek / Greece-focused landscape
- **No Greek-language shared dog-care or chore app found.** None of the apps checked lists Greek as a UI language: Cozi, FamilyWall, Tody, Sweepy, Puppr, Meal Time and Fed? all leave it out.
- Greek pet apps found are **social or adoption apps**: *Doggo* (Thessaloniki, dog-owner social network, lost-dog alerts) and *Your Pet* (adoption and lost pets).
- **Government:** The **pet.gov.gr** National Pet Register (Law 4830/2021) lets owners see microchip, sterilisation and rabies-vaccination status with expiry date in **gov.gr Wallet**. ⚠ We did not confirm whether it sends reminders. This is an opportunity: our vet/vaccine reminders can mirror the dates that Greek owners already know from the Wallet.
- **Market size:** About **14% of Greek households own a dog** (FEDIAF 2022), roughly 655K dogs. Dogs are the most popular pet.
