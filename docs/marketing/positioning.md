# Positioning

Owner: marketer · Draft v1 · 2026-10-12 · Based on [competitors.md](competitors.md) and the product brief

## 1. The gap (white space)
1. **Coordination, not logging.** Every pet tracker we found *logs* what already happened ("Fed by Sarah, 6:28"). None lets a family member **claim** an upcoming task before doing it ("I'll take the evening walk"). Chore apps have assignment, but they know nothing about dogs.
2. **Cross-platform households.** The closest pet trackers (Pawlo, Fed?, PiwPaw, PetPilot) are iOS-only and some sync through iCloud. Mixed iPhone/Android families get left out. This matters especially in Greece, where Android has a large share.
3. **No category leader.** The shared-dog-care niche is a long tail of new indie apps with fewer than 50 ratings each. The large players are either training apps (Dogo, Puppr) or general family organisers (Cozi, FamilyWall).
4. **Sharing behind a paywall.** Sweepy, Tody, Meal Time and Fed? all charge for multi-user sharing. Free household sharing is a clear way to stand out, since sharing is our core.
5. **Nothing in Greek.** No competitor we checked has a Greek UI. Greek-language apps for dogs are social or adoption apps (Doggo, Your Pet) or government records (pet.gov.gr / gov.gr Wallet).

## 2. Positioning statement
> **For** households who share a dog, **Dog Schedule is** the shared daily plan for the dog **that** lets everyone see, claim and tick off walks, meals and meds, so the dog is never double-fed or left waiting. **Unlike** pet loggers that only record what already happened, or family to-do apps that know nothing about dogs, **we** coordinate *before* the task happens, on iPhone and Android, with **free family sharing**.

**One-liner:** *Who's walking the dog? Now everyone knows.*

## 3. Differentiators
1. **Claim → Done.** Reserve a task before you do it, so two people never both feed the dog and nobody assumes someone else took the walk. *(Unique among the competitors we reviewed.)*
2. **Free family sharing, iOS and Android.** Invite the whole household at no cost; the dog's day syncs in real time on every phone.
3. **Built for dogs.** Recurring walks, meals, meds, grooming and vet visits. Templates come ready and need no setup like a generic to-do list.
4. **Reminders that know who is on it.** Nudge the person who claimed the task, and alert the household when a task is overdue and nobody has claimed it.
5. **Made for Greece first.** A native Greek UI, with Greek defaults for meal times and walks in the heat, and vaccine dates that match the gov.gr Wallet record. *(⚠ Wallet integration is an idea only; there is no API.)*

## 4. Target personas
| Persona | Situation | Pain | Hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Coordinator Parent** (35–50) | Family of 3–5, one dog, teenagers who promised to help | Constant "Did anyone feed Max?" messages; double feeding; walks skipped | One screen with the dog's day and who is on each task |
| **The Shared-Dog Couple / Flatmates** (25–40) | Two to three adults with different schedules | Coordinating walks around work over WhatsApp; meds given twice | Claim walks and get a reminder only when it's your turn |
| **Kids/teens (secondary)** | Kids assigned dog duties | Forget; no credit for what they did | Tick it off, everyone sees who did it (streaks are a later idea) |

## 5. App Store copy (draft)
- **Name:** Dog Schedule *(working name until the user decides. Shortlist in [naming.md](naming.md): DogDibs, Voltaki, Skilaki. None is legally cleared yet ⚠)*
- **Subtitle EN (30 characters max):** `Share your dog's daily routine` (30)
  - Alternatives: `Walks, meals & meds, together` (29) · `Who's walking the dog today?` (28)
- **Subtitle EL (30 characters max):** `Το πρόγραμμα του σκύλου, μαζί` (29)
  - Alternative: `Ποιος βγάζει τον σκύλο σήμερα;` (30)
- **Short description (EN, Google Play limit 80 characters):** `Share your dog's walks, meals & meds with family. Claim it, do it, done.` (72)
- **Promo text (EN, App Store):** Stop asking "did anyone feed the dog?". Dog Schedule gives your whole household one shared plan for walks, meals, meds and vet visits. Claim a task, mark it done, and everyone knows.

| Tody Premium+ Family | **$39.99** (⚠ assumed yearly) | Sharing tier |
| Cozi Gold | **≈ $39/yr** | Broad family organiser |
| FamilyWall Premium | $7.99/mo · **$44.99/yr** | Broad family organiser |
| Homey Premium | $4.99/mo · $49.99/yr ⚠ | Kids' chores |
| Puppr | $13.99/mo · $99.99/yr | Content ceiling for dog owners |

**Marketer's read:** Free household sharing is close to essential, because the direct rival is free and sharing is what we are. If we monetise, a premium tier around **€15–25/yr**, or a lifetime option around **€30–40**, fits the niche. We would charge for extras such as multiple dogs, history and insights, vet/med records, and widgets, not for adding family members. Greek storefront prices and willingness to pay are **⚠ unverified**; business should check them.
